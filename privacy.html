Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Controller

Operator of GTA6 Clipper, contact: gtaclipperacc@gmail.com

What data the App processes

  • TikTok account data: When an account is connected, the App receives an access token and basic profile info (open ID, display name) from TikTok. These are stored only locally on the operator's computer and used solely to upload videos to that account.
  • Video content: Public Twitch clips are downloaded, edited and uploaded. No viewer data is collected.

The App does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and does not sell or share data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to TikTok (for uploads) and Twitch (to retrieve public clips).

Retention

Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected. Revoking access in TikTok (Settings › Security › Apps and services) invalidates them immediately; local copies are then deleted.
